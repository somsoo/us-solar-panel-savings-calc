Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 30 percent federal solar tax credit (ITC) work?

The federal Residential Clean Energy Credit allows homeowners to deduct 30 percent of total solar equipment and installation costs directly from federal taxes.

What is the average payback period for residential solar panels?

Most homeowners reach complete financial breakeven between 6 and 10 years, depending on local electricity rates, sun hours, and state utility net metering policies.

How much can rooftop solar panels save over 25 years?

A typical 8 kW residential solar system saves between 30,000 and 60,000 dollars in cumulative electricity bills over a standard 25-year panel warranty lifespan.
